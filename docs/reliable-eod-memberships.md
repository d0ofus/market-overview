# Breadth membership source and observation dates

The runner retains all five independent populations. Nasdaq, NYSE and Overall share one [NasdaqTrader directory](https://www.nasdaqtrader.com/dynamic/SymDir/nasdaqtraded.txt) request. S&P uses the [public constituent proxy](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/datasets/s-and-p-500-companies/main/data/constituents.csv). Russell uses the [official IWM holdings export](https://www.ishares.com/us/products/239710/ishares-russell-2000-etf/latest-holdings.csv), with bounded discovery of another same-product issuer export if the primary response is malformed.

NasdaqTrader's file-creation date and IWM's holdings-as-of date are source dates. A dated snapshot collected later can support reconstruction of its stated date; its real collection timestamp remains separate. A later collection cannot make an older source snapshot appear newer, and a source date after the target session remains ineligible. Verification timestamps after the actual wall clock are invalid.

The S&P proxy contains no upstream effective date. New publications expose `sourceAsOfDate: null` and the actual `verifiedAt`. A download observed on September 11 cannot supply September 10's membership. The reader selects a matching earlier immutable observed version within the five-session limit, marked degraded; missing evidence produces a gap. Legacy versions that used a download date in the source-date column remain unchanged in storage. Publication metadata uses their actual stored observation timestamp and does not repeat the download date as an upstream date.

The September 11 preflight found four issuer class-share symbols (`BH A`, `CRD A`, `GEF B`, `MOG A`). The parser converts a space suffix to dotted provider notation only when the issuer's security name identifies the same share class, and preserves the original source symbol in member metadata. Unexplained identifiers remain source failures. Zero or missing issuer prices do not remove listed equities from coverage. Explicit contingent-value rights, escrow positions and issuer-marked non-market holdings remain separately diagnosed exclusions; the company name CVR Energy is not treated as a contingent-value right.

NasdaqTrader ETF and test-issue flags remain authoritative exclusions, alongside preferred-stock, warrant, unit, right and note descriptions. “Par value” describes share capital and does not disqualify common stock. Regression fixtures cover the five observed affected names: AVD, CMRE, FVRR, GDOT and SB.

Membership refreshes do not rewrite accepted versions or historical publications. A source failure retains only verified matching membership for at most five exchange sessions and blocks the affected universe after expiry. The shared ingestion population is recalculated from the full current catalog and all selected memberships; it is never trimmed to pass an earlier sizing plan.
