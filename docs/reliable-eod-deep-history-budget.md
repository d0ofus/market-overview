# Bounded deep-history work

Daily EOD ingestion still processes the full population with its 260-session indicator window. Existing archived observations remain available to readers. The deep-work allowance applies to automatic 520-session fills, rotating full-history rechecks, and explicit selected 520/1,400-session backfills. Retention continues to validate and prune the complete population independently of which securities receive deep work.

The reviewed maximum is **4 distinct securities and 2,500 requested or rechecked observations per UTC week**, beginning Monday at 00:00 UTC. Both limits must pass before a deep provider request or adjustment replacement. A request reserves every actual exchange-session date from the earliest requested or retained repair date through its target session. The 1,400-session selected depth does not cap an older retained repair span. A current approved storage-capacity check is also required before deep work starts.

The Ops `eod_rollout_evidence` row `eod-deep-history-week:<Monday date>` stores the claimed security/date unions. Atomic compare-and-swap prevents concurrent jobs from overspending. Retries and lost responses preserve reservations; deeper requests charge only additional dates. New weeks have independent reservations and inherit the previous traversal position, so unfinished later securities are not permanently displaced by the first names in the catalog. Complete histories outside the selected recheck rotation consume no claim.

Explicit jobs retain their original ticker selection and requested depth. When other claims exhaust the week's allowance, the run remains `retrying` at `deep-history-deferred`, with `history-budget-deferred` and the next UTC Monday in `next_attempt_at`. Its progress reports admitted securities, charged observations, and per-security deferrals. A single requested repair span exceeding the configured weekly limit, lacking required calendar evidence, or naming a security outside the authenticated capacity population instead stops at `failed` / `deep-history-capacity-required`, with no promised automatic retry date. An unmeasured identity has the explicit per-security reason `history-population-capacity-required` and makes no provider request. The original selection, retained history, and any pending repair fence remain intact for reviewed recovery. Unknown listing ages and provider gaps are not converted to structural exclusions.

`storeEodDeepHistoryPolicy` accepts immutable policy records for future UTC weeks through the existing admitted Ops connection. Limits may be lowered, or a prior reduction restored, only within the fixed 4-security / 2,500-observation maxima. A policy cannot rewrite an existing week or its claims. No additional Worker binding or market/history schema is introduced. The storage forecast uses the full maximum for every UTC week intersecting its actual future-session horizon; reduced runtime settings do not justify shrinking that approved forecast.
