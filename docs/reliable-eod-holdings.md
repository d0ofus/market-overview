# Holdings validation and recovery

Constituent reads remain stored-only. They validate cached lists before pricing them. A quarantined list returns no constituent rows, an explicit reason and stored-row count; the existing rows remain retained for diagnosis. Source effective dates, successful full collections and latest failed/partial attempts remain separate. An attempt cannot refresh a full snapshot's date, and absent source dates remain unverified. Holdings older than seven calendar days are visibly dated and stale.

Future effective dates are rejected against the New York calendar date. While the exact-session EOD catalog is pending, the endpoint still returns dated holdings with null prices and `eod-catalog-unavailable`; infrastructure failures remain errors. IBIT's synthetic Bitcoin exposure has no verified allocation percentage or source date and cannot receive the unrelated BTC ETF's quote, chart or equity catalog identity. A stock fund holding the BTC ETF remains an equity/fund holding when its name identifies that security.

Refreshes replace the complete list and seed eligible equity/fund symbols in one three-statement transaction using bounded JSON inputs. Lists are limited to10,000 members and the serialized batch inputs to2MB. An atomic count guard aborts if a concurrent writer increased the existing list beyond the admitted delete count. Admission includes constituent and symbol indexes; its larger transaction envelope retains the configured daily and rolling ceilings. No delete commits separately from its replacement.

The audited EATZ cache contained 717 rows from an issuer-wide export. Both CSV and XLSX parsers now require the matching fund/account when a consolidated export identifies one, and AdvisorShares files cannot fall through to an unfiltered generic parser. The issuer reported 24 holdings at December 2025; a conservative 100-row EATZ ceiling quarantines the contaminated cache. Implausible aggregate weights are also quarantined, never rescaled to look valid. Explicit `0.50%` remains 0.50 percentage points; formatted Excel fractions retain their percentage formatting before parsing. [Issuer's shareholder report](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1408970/000182912626001854/advisorshares_ncsrs.htm)

EATZ stopped trading April 30, 2026 and liquidated May 7. Its configured row and retained history remain present, but current holdings are unavailable and refreshes do not call providers. `getEtfLifecycle` supplies the same facts to the EOD pipeline. [Issuer's SEC supplement](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1408970/000182912626003345/advisorshares-eatz_497.htm), [OCC confirmation](https://infomemo.theocc.com/infomemos?number=58928)

GLD uses the official `US GLD Historical Archive` workbook, requiring the correct sheet, dated observations and positive reported gold ounces and trust NAV. It records physical gold exposure with the archive's actual effective date. The workbook does not report an allocation percentage, so weight remains null. SLV accepts identifiable silver commodity rows from the issuer's holdings file. Reserved physical IDs, exact bullion names and the issuer source jointly identify these assets. Neither the GOLD equity symbol nor the XAU index establishes physical ownership. Physical assets do not seed equities, receive stock quotes or display equity charts. [GLD issuer page and archive link](https://www.spdrgoldshares.com/usa/gld/)

At implementation verification, GLD's official archive was available with September 10, 2026 observations. SLV's CSV endpoint instead returned HTML with HTTP 200; that response is rejected. A physical trust never falls back to an unrelated equity list when its official source fails. Previously accepted rows and their full-success date remain intact.

After deployment, use the existing authenticated `POST /api/admin/etf/GLD/sync` (and equivalent ticker endpoint) or call `syncEtfConstituents(env, ticker)` with the real core `DB` binding. This workflow writes core holdings/status only; it needs no Alpaca secret and changes no market/history schema. Inspect `GET /api/etf/GLD/constituents` for a dated physical row with null weight/stock price, and EATZ for quarantine/lifecycle metadata. A failed source returns the prior dated snapshot or explicit unavailable state. A known-corrupt cache cannot be described as a validated last-good list.

Scheduled refreshes default to seven days, preserving any explicitly saved `staleDays` override. Eligibility uses the retained full snapshot and its source date; a recent attempt does not make old holdings current. Failed, partial or empty results wait at least six hours before another attempt. A normal full response with an unchanged old, missing or future source date is checked at most daily. Every eligible fund is ordered by its oldest attempt, with known liquidated funds excluded before the bounded selection, so repeated source failures cannot monopolize the refresh slice.

SSGA refreshes accept only the requested fund's official holdings files. The linked holdings workbook is tried first; NAV, premium/discount and other performance files are excluded. The workbook must identify the requested ticker, contain a holdings sheet and report one valid effective date. A missing date or wrong fund is a source failure and cannot replace the retained full list. One issuer attempt is limited to one page plus four distinct holdings files, eight seconds per request and 45 seconds overall; 403/429 stops that issuer immediately. The same SSGA attempt is not repeated through a second fallback branch. A partial fallback leaves the full snapshot's original date intact.

The September 11 investigation found the XLC page serving a valid dated XLSX while the derived CSV returned HTTP 404. The old link regex excluded the letter `s`, missed the actual download, and probed unrelated price-history files. Its blanket negative-weight check then rejected the issuer's small signed futures position; persisted source diagnostics confirmed that rejection before the duplicate issuer round exhausted the operator's request cap. The revised parser was checked against the downloaded official workbook: 27 reported positions, effective September 9, obtained in two requests (page and workbook). [Official XLC page](https://www.ssga.com/us/en/intermediary/etfs/state-street-communication-services-select-sector-spdr-etf-xlc), [official holdings workbook](https://www.ssga.com/library-content/products/fund-data/etfs/us/holdings-daily-us-en-xlc.xlsx).

Those 27 positions include 24 equities, a money-market fund, a US-dollar balance and a signed index future. The issuer used `-` for both the money-market fund and currency balance; the old deduplication silently lost one. The currency now uses its actual `USD` identity, while the money-market fund keeps the issuer's nontradable identifier. Reported weights remain unchanged. Futures require matching official SSGA provenance, a recognized contract name and agreeing root/expiry codes; literal issuer cash balances can also be signed. Negative equity weights remain invalid. Money-market, cash and derivative positions remain visible but are excluded from equity symbol seeding, quotes and charts. The regression fixture retains all 27 positions and their original weights.

The subsequent full-sector check found additional official contract names such as `XAB MATERIALS SEP26` and `XAK TECHNOLOGY SEP26`. These are recognized only for the matching sector, contract root, month/year and issuer source. Positive futures positions use the same checks, preventing successful XLE/XLV refreshes from attaching equity charts to their contracts. XLF's literal pound-sterling balance uses `GBP`; the symbol alone does not establish a currency identity. XLP's `CONTRA WALGREENS BOOTS` position retains the issuer's identifier `931CVR013` as a corporate-action position, including its zero reported weight. An unexplained `-` holding still fails validation. Corporate-action positions remain visible without equity quotes, charts or symbol seeding. The dated regression fixtures cover all eleven sector files, preserve every reported position and weight, and do not contain issuer footer prose.
